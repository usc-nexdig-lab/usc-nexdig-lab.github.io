Crop a photo

Frame a photo before uploading it in the content editor. The site crops every photo to a fixed shape on its own; do it here instead when the automatic crop would cut off something that matters, like a face at the edge of a group shot.

Drop a photo here, or choose one below.

Drag the photo to move it. Scroll over it to zoom.

Then:
  1. Open the entry in the content editor.
  2. Choose the photo field and upload the file you just downloaded.
  3. Save. The upload is re-encoded to WebP by the editor either way.
